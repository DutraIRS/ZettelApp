Zotero

ZettelApp connects to Zotero, the free reference manager, so you can cite references in your notes and write literature notes linked to the item, and its PDF, in your library.

Set up

  1. Use Zotero 7 or later and keep it running.
  2. In Zotero, open Settings ▸ Advanced and turn on "Allow other applications on this computer to communicate with Zotero".
  3. In ZettelApp, Tools ▸ Options ▸ Zotero ▸ Test connection should say Connected to Zotero.

ZettelApp reads your library through Zotero's local API on this computer (127.0.0.1, port 23119 by default). It only reads: it never changes your Zotero library, and nothing goes over the internet. Your personal library (My Library) is searched; group libraries are not, yet.

Cite a reference

Insert ▸ Zotero Reference…, or the books button in the editor toolbar, opens a search box. Type part of a title, an author or a year. With an empty search, your most recently modified items are listed. Select an item to see its details, then:

  • Insert citation (or double-click) adds an author–year citation that opens the item in Zotero:

    [Ebbinghaus (1885)](zotero://select/library/items/ABCD2345)

  • New literature note creates a note for the item, with:

    • the item's title;
    • a reference line (authors, year, title, publication, DOI);
    • links to Open in Zotero, Open PDF (when the item has a PDF attachment) and the DOI;
    • the abstract;
    • Key ideas and My thoughts sections to fill in;
    • the tag literature plus the item's Zotero tags;
    • the Zotero item as the note's source link.

Literature notes are the classic Zettelkasten bridge between reading and your own permanent notes: write the key ideas in your own words, then link them to the notes they support, extend or contradict.

Zotero links

Clicking a Zotero link in a note opens Zotero at the item, or opens the PDF in Zotero's reader. For safety, ZettelApp only follows two kinds of Zotero link and refuses any other zotero:// address:

zotero://select/library/items/<KEY>
zotero://open-pdf/library/items/<KEY>?page=<N>

Group-library links (zotero://select/groups/<ID>/items/<KEY>) are accepted too. You can also paste a Zotero link into a note's source link field. In Zotero, right-click an item and copy its link (or use an add-on such as Zutilo), then paste it.

Troubleshooting

Message What to do
Could not reach Zotero Start Zotero 7 or later. Check the port in Options ▸ Zotero (default 23119).
Zotero refused the request Turn on the Allow other applications… setting (step 2 above).
This Zotero version has no local API Update Zotero to version 7 or later.
